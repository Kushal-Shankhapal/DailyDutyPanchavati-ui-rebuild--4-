### 4\. What the Four Dates Actually Prove: Yesterday's Personnel as a Mutable Baseline

Comparing 10/09, 11/09, 12/09, and 16/09 conclusively resolves the fundamental architectural question regarding roster duplication:

* **Yesterday's Roster IS a Starting Baseline (COPY+DAILY EDIT)**:  
* A large portion of personnel remain in identical positions from 10/09 to 11/09 (e.g. वपोनि गजेंद्र पाटील as Senior PI, पोहवा 1452 लांडे as PSO, पोहवा 692 गुंबाडे as Hazari Master, पोहवा 1091 गारे as Property Clerk, पोशि 1243 महाले on Peter Mobile) 1\. This proves that **copying yesterday's finalized roster is the core primary mechanism for initializing today's draft** 13, 15, 25-27.  
* **Yesterday's Roster IS NOT Immutable Copied Data**:  
* On 11/09, पोनि सुनिल पवार goes on Weekly Off (साप्ताहिक सुट्टी) 11.md.md Table 4, so he is removed from the active Day PSO oversight slot, and पोनि नितीन आंधळे takes over 11.md.md Table 1\.  
* On 11/09, सपोनि प्रशांत बोरसे goes to High Court Mumbai (हायकोर्ट \- मुंबई) 11.md.md Table 4, so his Karanja Chowki slot is modified 11.md.md Table 1\.  
* On 16/09 (Ganeshotsav Bandobast), massive staffing re-allocations occur: सपोनि हेमंत मोहिते, सपोनि सतिश शिरसाठ, पोउनि कैलास जाधव, पोउनि प्रकाश नेमाणे, and पोउनि सचिन चव्हाण are deployed to श्री गणेशोत्सव बंदोबस्त on Page 3 & Page 5 16.md.md Table 3 & Table 10, forcing extensive daily replacements on Page 1 and Page 2 16.md.md Table 1 & Table 2\.  
* **Operational Conclusion**:  
* The four dates prove that yesterday's roster is copied as a **mutable draft baseline** 13, 15, 25, 26\. The system duplicates yesterday's structured assignments into today's draft, overlays today's known leaves and weekly offs (setting affected slots to vacant/RIKT), and presents the workspace to the Station Writer for daily editing and replacement 13, 25, 28-30.  
* Copied personnel assignments remain fully editable throughout the DRAFT state and are **never locked or immutable until today's chart itself is finalized** 25, 31-34.
